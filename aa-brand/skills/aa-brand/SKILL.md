Make it look like a page of https://artificialanalysis.ai, using the brand kit at https://artificialanalysis.ai/brand-kit. Look at the live site for anything not covered here.

- Fonts: Victor Serif Basic for large headings, Suisse Intl for everything else. `https://artificialanalysis.ai/fonts/VictorSerifBasic/VictorSerifBasic-{Medium,Semibold}.woff2`, `https://artificialanalysis.ai/fonts/SuisseIntl/SuisseIntl-{Light,Regular,Medium}.woff2`.
- Logos: `https://artificialanalysis.ai/img/brand-kit/{main,vertical,horizontal,icon}.svg`. Everything else, including PNGs: `https://artificialanalysiscdn.com/brand-kit/aa_brand_kit.zip`. Guidelines: `https://artificialanalysiscdn.com/brand-kit/aa_brand_guidelines.pdf`.
- Colours: black on white; greys `#F9F9F9 #E7E7E7 #D9D9D9 #959595 #777777 #4E4E4E #1E1E1E`; purple `#8842FD`, light purple `#C394FF`, dark purple `#741A66`, plum `#43003B`; accents lime `#CEFF73`, magenta `#E676FD`, mint `#B1FDEB`, yellow `#FDE002`, orange `#FF7734`, blue `#6C1BFD`.
- Names: "Artificial Analysis" in full once, then "AA". Keep index versions ("Intelligence Index v4.1"), the "-AA" suffix on adapted benchmarks, and a date on every figure. Charts carry "Source: Artificial Analysis" inside the frame.
